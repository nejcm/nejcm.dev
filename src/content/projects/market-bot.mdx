Turns public market data into research artifacts, including equity and crypto overviews, instrument briefs, and thematic research. It tracks observable forecasts and scores them against public observations, with a local Svelte console for browsing runs and calibration results.

The project is under active development. Its reports are research views, not trading advice or trade signals.
